Privacy policy

Neon Blast is a paid mobile arcade puzzle game. App id: com.byslave.neonpatlat. Developer: byslave.

What we collect

Neon Blast does not use ads, analytics SDKs, or a game server. If you join the optional local league, your email, a password hash, and player name stay only in this device’s storage (localStorage / app data). They are not sent to us, to advertisers, or to any third-party account system.

Game data

Score, combo, and sound preference are stored on this device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Ranks

Other names on the world list are stage racers. Your score belongs to this phone; there is no online league.

Internet

The web and store builds may load Google Fonts. Gameplay itself does not require an account or a network connection.

Children

The game is a casual puzzle suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. League sign-in is optional and stored only on the device.

Contact

Questions: use the email on the Google Play / App Store listing for Neon Blast.
