Privacy policy

BLOCK PATLAT is a mobile arcade puzzle game. App id: com.byslave.neonpatlat.

Data we keep

If you join the league, your email, password hash, and player name stay only in this device’s storage (localStorage). They are not sent to a server, ad network, or analytics service. The internet is used for Google Fonts and (in the web build) page files.

Game data

Score, combo, and sound preference are stored on this device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Ranks

Other names on the world list are stage racers. Your score belongs to this phone; there is no online league.

Contact

Developer: byslave. The email on the Play Console listing is the contact address.
