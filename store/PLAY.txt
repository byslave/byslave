APP NAME
Neon Blast

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 chars max)
Drag neon pieces, blast lines, stack combos. Arcade puzzle — no ads.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Neon Blast is a fast arcade puzzle. Drag a piece onto the 8×8 board, fill a row or column, and blast it. Blocks fall, chains keep going, and big combos set the cubes on fire.

• 8×8 neon board with gravity chains
• Combos heat up — 10x ignites the screen
• Stage cabinets unlock when you clear the whole board
• Optional local league with email on this device only
• No ads, no energy timers, no in-app shop

Play as a guest. Sign in on Ranks only if you want a name on this phone’s board.
